I am currently a 3rd year MS student at the [University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC)](https://www.uestc.edu.cn/), majoring in Computer Science. 

During 2019-2023, I conducted my undergraduate studies at the Glasgow College of the UESTC, specialising in Electronic Engineering. During this period, I have been awarded several first-class scholarships for **outstanding students**. In 2023, I started my MS degree in the Laboratory of [Intelligent Collaborative Computing](https://icct.uestc.edu.cn/index.htm). During this period, I was awarded the **National Scholarship** and the **University-level First-Class Scholarship**.

My research focuses on the *session-based recommendation* and *generative recommendation systems*. As the first author, I have published several high-quality papers in prestigious platform including **IJCAI** (CCF-A), **AAAI** (CCF-A) and **DSE** (JCR Q1). My research topics address core technical challenges in recommendation systems, specifically covering three key directions: *user intent mining*, *user behavior noise mitigation*, *and long-tail recommendation*.

From May to September 2025, I worked as a Recommendation Algorithm Intern at **ByteDance - TikTok**, where I deeply engaged in the optimization of core pipelines for recommendation systems. I led the fusion and enhancement of *multi-path recall* and took charge of the iterative upgrading of the *primary recall*.
